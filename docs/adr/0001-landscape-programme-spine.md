# Landscape section uses a programme-sector spine

The experimental landscape is primarily a parameter inventory, organised by programme
sector (solar/reactor → long-baseline accelerator → next-generation) with experiments and
their measurements under each heading, then a global-fit close (newest NuFIT release at
chapter freeze + leading directs, open questions, one NOvA handoff paragraph). The intro may
still make a parameter-provenance claim (measurement class / L/E / flavour tag); that claim
is intellectual framing, not the subsection outline. This was chosen over a pure
parameter-by-parameter body and over a pure currently-operating catalogue, so finished
experiments can still appear when they hold a leading direct constraint.
